Our Standards

Every resource on this site is based strictly on authenticated narrations from the Prophet ﷺ — primarily from Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī and Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim, with supplementary narrations only where graded Ṣaḥīḥ or Ḥasan by recognised scholars. No weak narrations. No speculation. No unsourced claims. Every reference is linked for independent verification. Content follows the understanding of Ahl al-Sunnah wa'l-Jamāʿah.
