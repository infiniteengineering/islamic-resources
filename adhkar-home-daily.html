Home & Daily Life Adhkār

Remembrances for entering home, eating, drinking, wearing clothes, and daily activities

Entering & Leaving Home
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 1 Entering Home
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَلَجْنَا ، وَبِسْمِ اللَّهِ خَرَجْنَا ، وَعَلَى اللَّهِ رَبِّنَا تَوَكَّلْنَا
Bismillāhi walajnā, wa bismillāhi kharajnā, wa 'alā Allāhi rabbinā tawakkalnā.
In the Name of Allah we enter, and in the Name of Allah we leave, and upon Allah our Lord we rely.
Source: Abu Dawud 5096 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī)
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 2 Leaving Home
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ ، تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللَّهِ ، لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ
Bismillāh, tawakkaltu 'alā Allāh, lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāh.
In the Name of Allah, I place my trust in Allah, and there is no might nor power except with Allah.
The Prophet ﷺ said: "When a man goes out of his house and says this, it will be said to him: 'You are guided, defended, and protected.' The devils will go far from him."
Source: Abu Dawud 5095 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī) · At-Tirmidhi 3426 — Ḥasan Ṣaḥīḥ
Eating & Drinking
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 3 Before Eating
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ
Bismillāh.
In the Name of Allah.
If one forgets to say Bismillah before eating, say when remembering: "Bismillāhi fī awwalihi wa ākhirih" (In the Name of Allah at its beginning and end).
Source: Abu Dawud 3767 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī) · At-Tirmidhi 1858 — Ṣaḥīḥ
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 4 After Eating
الحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَطْعَمَنِي هَذَا وَرَزَقَنِيهِ مِنْ غَيْرِ حَوْلٍ مِنِّي وَلَا قُوَّةٍ
Alḥamdu lillāhi-lladhī aṭ'amanī hādhā wa razaqanīhi min ghayri ḥawlin minnee wa lā quwwah.
All praise is for Allah Who fed me this and provided it for me without any might nor power from myself.
Whoever says this, his previous sins are forgiven.
Source: Abu Dawud 4023 — Ḥasan (al-Albānī) · At-Tirmidhi 3458 — Ḥasan
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 5 Guest for Host
اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ لَهُمْ فِيمَا رَزَقْتَهُمْ ، وَاغْفِرْ لَهُمْ وَارْحَمْهُمْ
Allāhumma bārik lahum fīmā razaqtahum, wa ghfir lahum wa rḥamhum.
O Allah, bless for them what You have provided them, and forgive them and have mercy upon them.
Source: Muslim 2042 — Ṣaḥīḥ
Wearing Clothes
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 6 Wearing New Clothes
الحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي كَسَانِي هَذَا وَرَزَقَنِيهِ مِنْ غَيْرِ حَوْلٍ مِنِّي وَلَا قُوَّةٍ
Alḥamdu lillāhi-lladhī kasānī hādhā wa razaqanīhi min ghayri ḥawlin minnee wa lā quwwah.
All praise is for Allah Who clothed me with this and provided it for me without any might nor power from myself.
Whoever says this, his previous sins are forgiven.
Source: Abu Dawud 4023 — Ḥasan (al-Albānī) · At-Tirmidhi 3458 — Ḥasan
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Dhikr · 7 Removing Clothes
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ
Bismillāh.
In the Name of Allah.
Say Bismillah when removing clothes to protect one's modesty from being seen by the jinn.
Source: At-Tirmidhi 606 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī)
Restroom
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 8 Entering Restroom
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الخُبُثِ وَالخَبَائِثِ
Allāhumma innī a'ūdhu bika mina-l-khubuthi wa-l-khabā'ith.
O Allah, I seek refuge in You from male and female devils.
Source: Al-Bukhari 142 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Muslim 375 — Ṣaḥīḥ
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 9 Leaving Restroom
غُفْرَانَكَ
Ghufrānak.
I seek Your forgiveness.
Source: Abu Dawud 30 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī) · At-Tirmidhi 7 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Ibn Majah 300 — Ṣaḥīḥ
Wuḍū'
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Dhikr · 10 Before Wuḍū'
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ
Bismillāh.
In the Name of Allah.
There is no wuḍū' for one who does not mention the Name of Allah.
Source: Abu Dawud 101 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī) · At-Tirmidhi 25 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Ibn Majah 397 — Ṣaḥīḥ
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 11 After Wuḍū'
أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ ، وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ
Ashhadu an lā ilāha illallāhu waḥdahu lā sharīka lah, wa ashhadu anna Muḥammadan 'abduhu wa rasūluh.
I bear witness that none has the right to be worshipped except Allah alone, without partner, and I bear witness that Muḥammad is His slave and Messenger.
The gates of Paradise will be opened for whoever says this.
Source: Muslim 234 — Ṣaḥīḥ
Sneezing
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 12 When Sneezing
الحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ
Alḥamdu lillāh.
All praise is for Allah.
Source: Al-Bukhari 6224 — Ṣaḥīḥ
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 13 Reply to One Who Sneezes
When someone sneezes and says "Alḥamdu lillāh," reply with:
يَرْحَمُكَ اللَّهُ
Yarḥamuka Allāh.
May Allah have mercy on you.
Source: Al-Bukhari 6224 — Ṣaḥīḥ
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 14 Sneezer's Response
When someone says "Yarḥamuka Allāh" to you, respond with:
يَهْدِيكُمُ اللَّهُ وَيُصْلِحُ بَالَكُمْ
Yahdīkumu Allāhu wa yuṣliḥu bālakum.
May Allah guide you and rectify your condition.
Source: Al-Bukhari 6224 — Ṣaḥīḥ
About This Collection
