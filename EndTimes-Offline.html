The Major Signs of the Hour

Ad-Dajjāl · Al-Mahdī · ʿĪsā ibn Maryam (عليه السلام)

📖 Strictly Ṣaḥīḥ & Authenticated Aḥādīth Only — Full References Provided
Introduction Ad-Dajjāl Al-Mahdī ʿĪsā (عليه السلام) Sequence Protection Sources
Section One

Introduction

Belief in the Signs of the Hour is part of belief in the Last Day — one of the six pillars of Īmān. The Prophet ﷺ warned his Ummah about these events so that the believers may be prepared and protected.

The Prophet ﷺ described the trial of Ad-Dajjāl as the greatest trial from the creation of Ādam until the Hour. Every prophet warned his nation about the one-eyed liar, and our Prophet ﷺ provided the most detailed descriptions to protect his Ummah.

This page presents only what is established through authenticated narrations from the Prophet ﷺ, primarily from Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī and Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim, with supplementary narrations from the Sunan collections where their chains have been verified as Ṣaḥīḥ (authentic) or Ḥasan (good) by recognised scholars of ḥadīth. Every narration is referenced so you can verify it yourself.

Methodology
  • Primary sources: Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī and Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim
  • Supplementary: Sunan Abī Dāwūd, Sunan Ibn Mājah, Jāmiʿ al-Tirmidhī — only where graded Ṣaḥīḥ or Ḥasan by recognised hadith scholars (e.g., al-Albānī and other specialists), and where the grading is accepted by the mainstream Sunni hadith tradition
  • No weak (Ḍaʿīf) narrations included
  • No speculation — only what the Prophet ﷺ stated
  • Sunni orthodoxy (Ahl al-Sunnah wa'l-Jamāʿah)
  • Where scholars differed in interpretation, the most widely accepted Sunni understanding is presented
Section Two

المسيح الدجّال Ad-Dajjāl — The False Messiah

The greatest trial to face mankind. The Prophet ﷺ spoke of him more than any other sign of the Hour, and every prophet warned their nation about him.

Every Prophet Warned of Him ▼

The Prophet ﷺ said: "No prophet was sent but that he warned his followers against the one-eyed liar (Ad-Dajjāl). Beware! He is blind in one eye, and your Lord is not so, and there will be written between his eyes (the word) Kāfir (i.e., disbeliever)."
Narrated by Anas ibn Mālik (رضي الله عنه)
Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 7131
The Prophet ﷺ said: "The Dajjāl is blind in one eye and there is written between his eyes the word 'Kāfir'." He then spelled the word as Kāf, Fā, Rā — which every Muslim would be able to read.
Narrated by Anas ibn Mālik (رضي الله عنه)
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2933b

His Physical Description ▼

The Prophet ﷺ provided detailed physical descriptions so the believers could recognise him and not be deceived:

The Prophet ﷺ said: "He (Dajjāl) would be a young man with twisted, contracted hair, and a blind eye. I compare him to ʿAbd al-ʿUzzā ibn Qaṭan."
From the lengthy ḥadīth of An-Nawwās ibn Samʿān (رضي الله عنه)
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2937a
The Prophet ﷺ said: "While sleeping near the Kaʿbah, I saw in my dream a man of brown complexion, the best one can see amongst brown-complexioned men, and his hair was long that it fell between his shoulders. His hair was lank and water was dribbling from his head... Then I asked who he was. They said, 'He is the Masīḥ (Messiah), Ad-Dajjāl.'"
Narrated by ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿUmar (رضي الله عنهما)
Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 7128
The Prophet ﷺ said about the Dajjāl: "He is blind in the left eye and has a great quantity of hair."
Narrated by Ḥudhayfa (رضي الله عنه)
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2934
Summary of His Description (from Ṣaḥīḥ narrations)
  • A young man with curly/twisted hair
  • Blind in one eye (his right eye likened to a floating grape in some narrations; his left eye described as defective in others — scholars reconcile that both eyes are defective, one blind and one damaged)
  • The word "Kāfir" (كَافِر) written between his eyes, recognised by every believer — even those unable to read
  • It is reported in authentic narrations that he will have no children
  • He is a human being who will eat and drink — Allāh is far above that

His Emergence and Trials ▼

"He would appear on the way between Syria and Iraq and would spread mischief right and left. O servants of Allāh! Adhere (to the path of Truth)."

We said: "O Messenger of Allāh, how long would he stay on the earth?" He ﷺ said: "For forty days — one day like a year, and one day like a month, and one day like a week, and the rest of the days would be like your days."

We said: "O Messenger of Allāh, would one day's prayer suffice for the prayers of a day equal to one year?" He ﷺ said: "No, but you must make an estimate of time (and then observe prayer)."

We said: "How quickly would he walk upon the earth?" He ﷺ said: "Like cloud driven by the wind."
From the ḥadīth of An-Nawwās ibn Samʿān (رضي الله عنه)
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2937a
The Prophet ﷺ said that the Dajjāl would have water and fire with him — what would seem to be fire would be cold water, and what would seem to be water would be fire. He ﷺ warned: "So do not destroy yourselves."
Narrated by Ḥudhayfa (رضي الله عنه)
Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 7130
The Prophet ﷺ said: "He will come to the people and invite them (to follow him), and they will affirm their faith in him and respond to him. He will then give a command to the sky, and there will be rainfall upon the earth, and it will bring forth vegetation... Then he will call a person brimming with youth, and strike him with the sword and cut him into two pieces... He will then call that young man, and he will come forward laughing with his face gleaming."
From the ḥadīth of An-Nawwās ibn Samʿān (رضي الله عنه)
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2937a

He Cannot Enter Makkah or Madīnah ▼

The Prophet ﷺ said: "There will be no town which Ad-Dajjāl will not enter except Makkah and Madīnah, and there will be no entrance (road) but the angels will be standing in rows guarding it against him. Then Madīnah will shake with its inhabitants thrice, and Allāh will expel all the disbelievers and the hypocrites from it."
Narrated by Anas ibn Mālik (رضي الله عنه)
Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 1881
The Prophet ﷺ said: "The terror caused by Al-Masīḥ Ad-Dajjāl will not enter Madīnah, and at that time Madīnah will have seven gates, and there will be two angels at each gate guarding them."
Narrated by Abū Bakra (رضي الله عنه)
Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 7125

The Story of Tamīm al-Dārī and Al-Jassāsah ▼

This is the only ḥadīth in which the Prophet ﷺ narrated from a Companion's account and confirmed it. Tamīm al-Dārī (رضي الله عنه), a former Christian who accepted Islam, told the Prophet ﷺ that he had sailed on the sea and was driven by a storm to an island where they encountered a beast (Al-Jassāsah — the Spy) who led them to a man chained in a monastery.

The chained man (the Dajjāl) asked them about the date-palms of Baysān, the Lake of Ṭabariyyah (Tiberias), and the Spring of Zughar — inquiring whether they still had water and produced fruit. When they confirmed these things, he said: "Soon I will be given permission to emerge, and I will emerge and travel throughout the land. I will not leave any town but I will enter it within forty nights — apart from Makkah and Ṭaybah (Madīnah), for they are both forbidden to me. Every time I want to enter one of them, I will be met by an angel with an unsheathed sword in his hand, who will drive me back."

The Prophet ﷺ then said, striking the minbar with his stick: "This is Ṭaybah, this is Ṭaybah, this is Ṭaybah" — meaning Madīnah — "Have I not told you that? I liked what Tamīm said because it agreed with what I have been telling you about him and about Makkah and Madīnah."
Narrated by Fāṭimah bint Qays (رضي الله عنها)
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2942
Section Three

المهدي Al-Mahdī — The Guided One

A righteous leader from the family of the Prophet ﷺ who will appear near the end of time and fill the earth with justice as it was filled with oppression.

A Note on the Mahdī Aḥādīth

The ḥadīth about Al-Mahdī are not found explicitly by the title "Mahdī" in Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī or Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim. However, Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim contains narrations that scholars understand to refer to him (the leader behind whom ʿĪsā will pray). The explicit Mahdī narrations are found in the Sunan collections (Abū Dāwūd, Ibn Mājah, al-Tirmidhī) and Musnad Aḥmad, and many scholars — including Ibn Taymiyyah, al-Albānī, and the scholars of the Permanent Committee (al-Lajnah al-Dāʾimah) — have graded the core narrations as authentic. Scholars such as al-Sakhāwī, Ibn Kathīr, and al-Shawkānī stated that the reports about the Mahdī are transmitted through numerous chains and widely accepted by the scholars of Ahl al-Sunnah.

His Lineage and Name ▼

The Prophet ﷺ said: "The Mahdī will be of my family, of the descendants of Fāṭimah."
Narrated by Umm Salamah (رضي الله عنها)
Sunan Abī Dāwūd 4284 — Graded Ṣaḥīḥ by Ibn Taymiyyah & al-Albānī
The Prophet ﷺ said: "The world will not pass away until the Arabs are ruled by a man from my family whose name agrees with my name."
Narrated by ʿAbdullāh ibn Masʿūd (رضي الله عنه)
Sunan Abī Dāwūd 4282 — Graded Ṣaḥīḥ by al-Albānī
The Prophet ﷺ said: "The Mahdī will be of my stock, and will have a broad forehead and a prominent nose. He will fill the earth with equity and justice as it was filled with oppression and tyranny, and he will rule for seven years."
Narrated by Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī (رضي الله عنه)
Sunan Abī Dāwūd 4285 — Graded Ḥasan by al-Albānī

The Circumstances of His Appearance ▼

The Prophet ﷺ said: "The Mahdī is from us, the people of the household (Ahl al-Bayt). Allāh will set right his affairs in a single night."
Narrated by ʿAlī ibn Abī Ṭālib (رضي الله عنه)
Sunan Ibn Mājah 4085 — Graded Ḥasan by al-Albānī
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Disagreement will occur at the death of a Caliph, and a man of the people of Madīnah will come fleeing to Makkah. Some of the people of Makkah will come to him, bring him out against his will, and swear allegiance to him between the Corner (of the Kaʿbah) and the Maqām (of Ibrāhīm). An expeditionary force will then be sent against him from Shām (Syria), but will be swallowed up in the desert between Makkah and Madīnah."
Narrated by Umm Salamah (رضي الله عنها)
Sunan Abī Dāwūd 4286 — Graded Ḥasan by al-Albānī

ʿĪsā (عليه السلام) Will Pray Behind Him ▼

This narration, from Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim itself, is understood by the scholars of Ahl al-Sunnah to refer to Al-Mahdī — the Imām from among this Ummah behind whom ʿĪsā (عليه السلام) will pray:

The Prophet ﷺ said: "A section of my people will not cease fighting for the Truth and will prevail till the Day of Resurrection." He ﷺ said: "ʿĪsā son of Maryam would then descend, and their (the Muslims') commander would invite him to come and lead them in prayer, but he would say: 'No, some amongst you are commanders over some (amongst you).' This is the honour from Allāh for this Ummah."
Narrated by Jābir ibn ʿAbdullāh (رضي الله عنهما)
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 156
The Prophet ﷺ said: "How will you be when the son of Maryam descends amongst you and your Imām is from among you?"
Narrated by Abū Hurayrah (رضي الله عنه)
Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 3449 & Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 155d
Section Four

عيسى ابن مريم عليه السلام ʿĪsā ibn Maryam — The Messiah

The true Messiah, a Prophet and Messenger of Allāh, who was raised alive to the heavens and will return to the earth before the Hour.

The Qur'ānic Confirmation ▼

وَإِنَّهُۥ لَعِلۡمٞ لِّلسَّاعَةِ فَلَا تَمۡتَرُنَّ بِهَا وَٱتَّبِعُونِۚ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطٞ مُّسۡتَقِيمٞ
"And indeed, he (ʿĪsā) is a sign for knowledge of the Hour, so be not in doubt of it, and follow Me. This is a straight path."
Sūrah al-Zukhruf (43:61)
وَإِن مِّنۡ أَهۡلِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ إِلَّا لَيُؤۡمِنَنَّ بِهِۦ قَبۡلَ مَوۡتِهِۦۖ وَيَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ يَكُونُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ شَهِيدٗا
"And there is none from the People of the Scripture but that he will surely believe in him (ʿĪsā) before his death. And on the Day of Resurrection he will be against them a witness."
Sūrah al-Nisāʾ (4:159)

His Descent ▼

"...and it would be at this very time that Allāh would send ʿĪsā, son of Maryam, and he will descend at the white minaret in the eastern side of Damascus wearing two garments lightly dyed with saffron, placing his hands on the wings of two Angels. When he would lower his head, there would fall beads of perspiration from his head, and when he would raise it up, beads like pearls would scatter from it."
From the ḥadīth of An-Nawwās ibn Samʿān (رضي الله عنه)
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2937a

He Will Kill Ad-Dajjāl ▼

"Every non-believer who would smell the odour of his (ʿĪsā's) self would die, and his breath would reach as far as he would be able to see. He would then search for him (Dajjāl) until he would catch hold of him at the gate of Ludd and would kill him."
From the ḥadīth of An-Nawwās ibn Samʿān (رضي الله عنه)
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2937a

His Mission on Earth ▼

The Prophet ﷺ said: "By Him in Whose hand is my life, the son of Maryam will soon descend among you as a just judge. He will break crosses, kill swine and abolish the Jizyah, and wealth will pour forth to such an extent that no one will accept it."
Narrated by Abū Hurayrah (رضي الله عنه)
Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 2222 & Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 155a
The Prophet ﷺ said: "I swear by Allāh that the son of Maryam will certainly descend as a just judge and he would definitely break the cross, and kill swine and abolish the Jizyah, and would leave the young she-camel and no one would endeavour to collect Zakāt on it. Spite, mutual hatred and jealousy against one another will certainly disappear, and when he summons people to accept wealth, not even one would do so."
Narrated by Abū Hurayrah (رضي الله عنه)
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 155c

ʿĪsā (عليه السلام) will rule as a just leader among the Muslims. The scholars of Ahl al-Sunnah explain from these narrations that he will judge according to the law of Islam and will not bring a new revelation.

Ya'jūj and Ma'jūj (Gog and Magog) ▼

After killing the Dajjāl, ʿĪsā (عليه السلام) and the believers will face the emergence of Ya'jūj and Ma'jūj:

"Allāh would then reveal to ʿĪsā: 'I have released servants of Mine whom no one will be able to fight. Take My servants to safety in al-Ṭūr (Mount Sinai).' And Allāh would send Ya'jūj and Ma'jūj, who will come swarming down from every mound. The first of them will pass by Lake Ṭabariyyah (Tiberias) and will drink the water in it, and the last of them will pass by it and will say: 'There was water here once.'"

"Then ʿĪsā and his companions will make duʿā to Allāh, and Allāh will send insects (worms) that will attack their (Ya'jūj and Ma'jūj's) necks, and in the morning they will all be dead as one."
From the ḥadīth of An-Nawwās ibn Samʿān (رضي الله عنه)
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2937a

His Life and Death ▼

ʿĪsā (عليه السلام) will live on earth after his descent and then die a natural death. The Muslims will pray the funeral prayer over him.

The Prophet ﷺ said: "ʿĪsā ibn Maryam will then live on the earth for forty years, and then he will die, and the Muslims will pray over him."
Narrated by Abū Hurayrah (رضي الله عنه)
Sunan Abī Dāwūd 4324 — Graded Ṣaḥīḥ by al-Albānī
Section Five

The Sequence of Events

Based on what can be derived from the authentic narrations. Scholars note that no explicit ordering of all events was narrated from the Prophet ﷺ in a single ḥadīth, but the following order reflects the most widely accepted understanding among Sunni scholars based on the collective narrations. Some details of precise sequencing were not explicitly stated in a single narration.

1
The Appearance of Al-Mahdī He will emerge at a time of great corruption and oppression. He will be given bay'ah (allegiance) between the Corner and the Maqām in Makkah.
Sunan Abī Dāwūd 4286
2
The Emergence of Ad-Dajjāl He will appear between Shām and Iraq, and will travel across the earth for forty days (one like a year, one like a month, one like a week, and the rest like normal days).
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2937a
3
The Descent of ʿĪsā (عليه السلام) He will descend at the white minaret, east of Damascus, at the time of Fajr prayer. The Muslims' Imām (Al-Mahdī) will offer to step back, but ʿĪsā will pray behind him — an honour for this Ummah.
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2937a, Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 156
4
The Killing of Ad-Dajjāl ʿĪsā (عليه السلام) will pursue Ad-Dajjāl and kill him at the gate of Ludd (in Palestine).
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2937a
5
The Release of Ya'jūj and Ma'jūj Allāh will reveal to ʿĪsā to take the believers to Mount Sinai. Ya'jūj and Ma'jūj will spread across the earth, then Allāh will destroy them.
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2937a
6
An Era of Peace and Justice ʿĪsā (عليه السلام) will rule the earth with justice. Spite, hatred, and jealousy will disappear. Wealth will be so abundant no one will accept charity.
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 155c, Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 2222
7
The Death of ʿĪsā (عليه السلام) He will live for forty years on earth, then die a natural death. The Muslims will pray over him.
Sunan Abī Dāwūd 4324
Section Six

Protection from Ad-Dajjāl

The Prophet ﷺ taught his Ummah specific means of protection from the greatest trial to face mankind.

1. Reciting Sūrah al-Kahf

The Prophet ﷺ said: "He who amongst you would survive to see him (the Dajjāl) should recite over him the opening verses of Sūrah al-Kahf."
From the ḥadīth of An-Nawwās ibn Samʿān (رضي الله عنه)
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2937a
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever memorises ten āyāt from the beginning of Sūrah al-Kahf will be protected from the Dajjāl."
Narrated by Abū al-Dardāʾ (رضي الله عنه)
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 809a

2. Seeking Refuge in Every Prayer

Duʿā After Tashahhud
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ عَذَابِ الْقَبْرِ وَمِنْ عَذَابِ النَّارِ وَمِنْ فِتْنَةِ الْمَحْيَا وَالْمَمَاتِ وَمِنْ فِتْنَةِ الْمَسِيحِ الدَّجَّالِ
Allāhumma innī aʿūdhu bika min ʿadhāb al-qabr, wa min ʿadhāb al-nār, wa min fitnat al-maḥyā wa'l-mamāt, wa min fitnat al-masīḥ al-Dajjāl.
"O Allāh, I seek refuge with You from the punishment of the grave, from the punishment of the Fire, from the trials of life and death, and from the trial of the False Messiah (Al-Masīḥ Ad-Dajjāl)."
Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 1377 & Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 588

The Prophet ﷺ used to seek refuge from the trial of the Dajjāl in every prayer, and he instructed his Ummah to do the same. ʿĀʾishah (رضي الله عنها) reported that the Prophet ﷺ used to make this supplication in his Ṣalāh.

3. Staying Away from Him

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Let him who hears of the Dajjāl go far from him, for by Allāh, a man will come to him thinking he is a believer, but will end up following him because of the doubts he sends."
Narrated by ʿImrān ibn Ḥuṣayn (رضي الله عنه)
Sunan Abī Dāwūd 4319 — Graded Ṣaḥīḥ by al-Albānī

4. Knowing He is One-Eyed — Your Lord is Not

The Prophet ﷺ said: "I warn you of him, and there was no prophet but warned his followers of him; but I will tell you something about him which no prophet has told his followers: Ad-Dajjāl is one-eyed whereas Allāh is not."
Narrated by ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿUmar (رضي الله عنهما)
Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 7127
Summary of Protections (from Ṣaḥīḥ narrations)
  • Memorise and recite the opening (or closing) ten āyāt of Sūrah al-Kahf
  • Seek refuge from the Dajjāl in every prayer (after Tashahhud)
  • Stay far away from the Dajjāl if he appears
  • Know Allāh is not one-eyed; the Dajjāl is one-eyed with "Kāfir" written between his eyes
  • Know that no one can see their Lord until they die — the Dajjāl will be seen by all
  • Know the Dajjāl is a human who eats and drinks — Allāh is far above that
  • Makkah and Madīnah are protected from his entry, as stated in the authentic narrations
