Witr Prayer Adhkār

The odd-numbered prayer that concludes the night — guidance and supplications

The Practice of Witr
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Guidance · 1 Every Night
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Witr is a duty upon every Muslim. Whoever wishes may pray five rakʿahs, whoever wishes may pray three, and whoever wishes may pray one." He ﷺ also said: "Make Witr the last of your prayer at night."
Source: Abu Dawud 1422 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī) · An-Nasa'i 1712 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī)
Al-Bukhari 998 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Muslim 751 — Ṣaḥīḥ
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Guidance · 2 Number of Rakʿahs
Multiple authentic reports exist regarding the number of rakʿahs for Witr:
• One rakʿah — The minimum
• Three rakʿahs — Very common
• Five rakʿahs — Authentic
• Seven rakʿahs — Authentic
• Nine rakʿahs — Authentic
• Eleven rakʿahs — The Prophet's ﷺ most common practice
All of these are established in the Sunnah. The worshipper may choose according to ability and circumstances.
Source: Muslim 738 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Abu Dawud 1422 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī)
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Guidance · 3 Timing
Witr is prayed after the ʿIshāʾ prayer and before Fajr. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah has added a prayer for you, which is Witr, so pray it between ʿIshāʾ and Fajr." The last third of the night is the most virtuous time, but it may be prayed immediately after ʿIshāʾ.
Source: At-Tirmidhi 452 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Ahmad 21459 — Ṣaḥīḥ (Shuʿayb al-Arnaʾūṭ)
Qunūt of Witr
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Guidance · 4 Qunūt Timing
Qunūt is recited in the last rakʿah of Witr, after completing the recitation and before going into rukūʿ (bowing). Some scholars also allow it after rising from rukūʿ. Both positions are based on authentic reports.
Source: An-Nasa'i 1746 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī) · Ibn Majah 1182 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī)
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 5 Qunūt — Primary Wording
This wording is authentically reported. Other authentic wordings also exist and are used by scholars. The Prophet ﷺ taught this supplication to al-Ḥasan ibn ʿAlī رضي الله عنهما.
اللَّهُمَّ اهْدِنِي فِيمَنْ هَدَيْتَ ، وَعَافِنِي فِيمَنْ عَافَيْتَ ، وَتَوَلَّنِي فِيمَنْ تَوَلَّيْتَ ، وَبَارِكْ لِي فِيمَا أَعْطَيْتَ ، وَقِنِي شَرَّ مَا قَضَيْتَ ، فَإِنَّكَ تَقْضِي وَلَا يُقْضَى عَلَيْكَ ، وَإِنَّهُ لَا يَذِلُّ مَنْ وَالَيْتَ ، وَلَا يَعِزُّ مَنْ عَادَيْتَ ، تَبَارَكْتَ رَبَّنَا وَتَعَالَيْتَ
Allāhumma hdinī fīman hadayt, wa 'āfinī fīman 'āfayt, wa tawallanī fīman tawallayt, wa bārik lī fīmā a'ṭayt, wa qinī sharra mā qaḍayt, fa-innaka taqḍī wa lā yuqḍā 'alayk, wa innahu lā yadhillu man wālayt, wa lā ya'izzu man 'ādayt, tabārakta rabbanā wa ta'ālayt.
O Allah, guide me among those You have guided, pardon me among those You have pardoned, be an ally to me among those to whom You are an ally, and bless for me what You have bestowed. Protect me from the evil You have decreed, for indeed You decree and none can decree over You. He whom You support can never be humiliated, and he whom You oppose can never be honored. Blessed are You, our Lord, and Exalted.
Source: Abu Dawud 1425 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī) · At-Tirmidhi 464 — Ṣaḥīḥ · An-Nasa'i 1745 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī)
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 6 Addition After Qunūt
This addition is reported to be recited after the main Qunūt supplication:
اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا نَسْتَعِينُكَ وَنَسْتَغْفِرُكَ ، وَنُثْنِي عَلَيْكَ الْخَيْرَ ، وَلَا نَكْفُرُكَ ، وَنَخْلَعُ وَنَتْرُكُ مَنْ يَفْجُرُكَ ، اللَّهُمَّ إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ ، وَلَكَ نُصَلِّي وَنَسْجُدُ ، وَإِلَيْكَ نَسْعَى وَنَحْفِدُ ، نَرْجُو رَحْمَتَكَ وَنَخْشَى عَذَابَكَ ، إِنَّ عَذَابَكَ بِالْكُفَّارِ مُلْحِقٌ
Allāhumma innā nasta'īnuka wa nastaghfiruk, wa nuthnī 'alayka-l-khayr, wa lā nakfuruk, wa nakhla'u wa natruku man yafjuruk. Allāhumma iyyāka na'bud, wa laka nuṣallī wa nasjud, wa ilayka nas'ā wa naḥfid, narjū raḥmatak wa nakhshā 'adhābak, inna 'adhābaka bi-l-kuffāri mulḥiq.
O Allah, we seek Your help and Your forgiveness, and we praise You with all good and do not deny You. We renounce and abandon whoever disobeys You. O Allah, You alone we worship, to You we pray and prostrate, and towards You we strive and hasten. We hope for Your mercy and fear Your punishment, verily Your punishment will surely reach the disbelievers.
Source: Authenticated in narrations from ʿUmar رضي الله عنه — Al-Bayhaqi 2/210 — Graded Ḥasan by some scholars
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Dhikr · 7 After Qunūt (Optional)
Some scholars recommend sending ṣalawāt (blessings) upon the Prophet ﷺ after Qunūt before going into rukūʿ:
اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ
Allāhumma ṣalli 'alā Muḥammad wa 'alā āli Muḥammad.
O Allah, send prayers upon Muḥammad and upon the family of Muḥammad.
Source: Based on the practice of some of the Salaf
After Witr
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 8 After Salām from Witr
The Prophet ﷺ would say after the salām of Witr:
سُبْحَانَ الْمَلِكِ الْقُدُّوسِ
Subḥāna-l-maliki-l-quddūs.
How perfect is the King, the Most Holy.
He ﷺ would repeat this three times, elongating the last repetition and raising his voice.
Source: An-Nasa'i 1699 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī) · Abu Dawud 1430 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī)
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 9 Addition After Witr
This is an authentic addition to be said after "Subḥāna-l-maliki-l-quddūs":
سُبْحَانَ الْمَلِكِ الْقُدُّوسِ رَبِّ الْمَلَائِكَةِ وَالرُّوحِ
Subḥāna-l-maliki-l-quddūs, rabbi-l-malā'ikati wa-r-rūḥ.
How perfect is the King, the Most Holy, Lord of the angels and the Spirit.
Source: An-Nasa'i 1730 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī) · Ad-Daraqutni 2/31 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī)
Additional Guidance
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Guidance · 10 If Witr is Missed
If someone sleeps through Witr or forgets to pray it, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Let him pray it when he remembers." However, it is prayed as an even number (Shaf') rather than odd. For example, if one usually prays three rakʿahs for Witr, make it up as four rakʿahs (two sets of two).
Source: Muslim 746 — Ṣaḥīḥ · At-Tirmidhi 467 — Ṣaḥīḥ
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Guidance · 11 One Witr Per Night
The Prophet ﷺ said: "There are not two Witr prayers in one night." This means if someone prays Witr early in the night, then wakes to pray Tahajjud, they should not pray Witr again — Witr has already been completed.
Source: Abu Dawud 1439 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī) · At-Tirmidhi 470 — Ṣaḥīḥ
About This Collection

Variations exist in wording, frequency, and timing across authentic narrations. This collection presents widely accepted reports transmitted from the Prophet ﷺ and used by major scholars of hadith and Sunnah.
