About This Collection

This is a personal compilation of authentic daily adhkār based on recognised hadith sources and authenticity gradings by established scholars. The material was organised through independent study with AI-assisted structuring and cross-referencing. This is not scholarly work or formal religious guidance. Unintentional errors may exist. Corrections are welcomed.

About Variations

Variations exist in wording, frequency, and timing across authentic narrations. This collection presents widely accepted reports transmitted from the Prophet ﷺ and used by major scholars of hadith and Sunnah. Where multiple authentic wordings exist, we have chosen commonly cited versions.

وَإِذَا سَأَلَكَ عِبَادِي عَنِّي فَإِنِّي قَرِيبٌ ۖ أُجِيبُ دَعْوَةَ ٱلدَّاعِ إِذَا دَعَانِ

"And when My servants ask you about Me — I am near. I respond to the call of the one who calls upon Me." — al-Baqarah (2:186)
