The 99 Names of Allah

Learn, understand, and call upon Allah by His Most Beautiful Names

Virtue of Learning the Names
إِنَّ لِلَّهِ تِسْعَةً وَتِسْعِينَ اسْمًا مِائَةً إِلَّا وَاحِدًا مَنْ أَحْصَاهَا دَخَلَ الْجَنَّةَ
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah has ninety-nine names — one hundred minus one. Whoever preserves them will enter Paradise."
Bukhari 7392 & Muslim 2677
What does 'preserve' mean? Scholars explain it means: (1) memorizing them, (2) understanding their meanings, (3) worshipping Allah by them, and (4) calling upon Him through them in supplication.
How to use this page: Click on any name to see its full meaning and explanation. Take time to reflect on each name and how it relates to Allah's perfection.
