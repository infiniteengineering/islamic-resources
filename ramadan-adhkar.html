أَذْكَارُ رَمَضَان

Ramadan Adhkar

Created by Farhan Shaikh — A simple reminder, with the help of AI

Every effort has been made to ensure accuracy and authenticity from the Quran and Sunnah. This guide reflects mainstream Sunni scholarship (Ahl al-Sunnah wa'l-Jamāʿah). If you find any errors, please forgive me and bring them to my attention so they can be corrected. May Allah reward you.

✧

اللَّهُمَّ فَرِّجْ هَمَّ الْمَهْمُومِينَ مِنَ الْمُسْلِمِينَ، وَنَفِّسْ كَرْبَ الْمَكْرُوبِينَ، وَاقْضِ الدَّيْنَ عَنِ الْمَدِينِينَ، وَاشْفِ مَرْضَانَا وَمَرْضَى الْمُسْلِمِينَ، وَارْحَمْ مَوْتَانَا وَمَوْتَى الْمُسْلِمِينَ، وَانْصُرْ إِخْوَانَنَا الْمُسْتَضْعَفِينَ فِي كُلِّ مَكَانٍ

"O Allah, relieve the distress of the distressed among the Muslims, remove the hardship of those in hardship, settle the debts of those in debt, heal our sick and the sick among the Muslims, have mercy on our deceased and the deceased among the Muslims, and grant victory to our oppressed brothers and sisters everywhere."

May you stay blessed and may your efforts be accepted. آمين

Suhoor (Pre-Dawn Meal)

▼
Virtue: The Prophet ﷺ said: "Eat suhoor, for in suhoor there is blessing." (Bukhari & Muslim)
Before Eating
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ
Bismillah
"In the name of Allah."
Bukhari & Muslim
If You Forget to Say Bismillah
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ أَوَّلَهُ وَآخِرَهُ
Bismillahi awwalahu wa aakhirahu
"In the name of Allah at the beginning and at the end."
Abu Dawud & Tirmidhi — Sahih
After Eating
الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَطْعَمَنِي هَذَا وَرَزَقَنِيهِ مِنْ غَيْرِ حَوْلٍ مِنِّي وَلَا قُوَّةٍ
Alhamdu lillahil-ladhi at'amani hadha wa razaqanihi min ghayri hawlin minni wa la quwwah
"Praise be to Allah Who has fed me this and provided it for me without any strength or power on my part."
Abu Dawud & Tirmidhi — Hasan
Intention for Fasting
نَوَيْتُ أَنْ أَصُومَ غَدًا مِنْ شَهْرِ رَمَضَانَ
Nawaytu an asuma ghadan min shahri Ramadan
"I intend to fast tomorrow for the month of Ramadan."
Note: The intention is made in the heart. Verbalising is permissible but not required or from the Sunnah. Forming intention at night is required for obligatory fasts according to many scholars.

Iftar (Breaking the Fast)

▼
Virtue: The Prophet ﷺ said: "The fasting person has a supplication that is answered when he breaks his fast." (Ibn Majah — Hasan)
When Breaking the Fast
ذَهَبَ الظَّمَأُ وَابْتَلَّتِ الْعُرُوقُ وَثَبَتَ الْأَجْرُ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ
Dhahaba adh-dhama'u wabtallatil-'urooqu wa thabatal-ajru in sha Allah
"The thirst is gone, the veins are moistened, and the reward is confirmed, if Allah wills."
Abu Dawud — Hasan
General Dua at Iftar
اللَّهُمَّ لَكَ صُمْتُ وَعَلَى رِزْقِكَ أَفْطَرْتُ
Allahumma laka sumtu wa 'ala rizqika aftartu
"O Allah, for You I have fasted and upon Your provision I have broken my fast."
Abu Dawud — Mursal (weak chain, but widely practised). Some scholars permit acting upon weak hadith in virtues of deeds when they are not fabricated and do not establish rulings.
Dua When Invited for Iftar
أَفْطَرَ عِنْدَكُمُ الصَّائِمُونَ وَأَكَلَ طَعَامَكُمُ الْأَبْرَارُ وَصَلَّتْ عَلَيْكُمُ الْمَلَائِكَةُ
Aftara 'indakumus-saa'imoon, wa akala ta'aamakumul-abraar, wa sallat 'alaykumul-malaa'ikah
"May the fasting break their fast with you, may the righteous eat your food, and may the angels send blessings upon you."
Abu Dawud — Sahih

Declaration of Tawheed
لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ

"There is no god but Allah alone, with no partner. His is the dominion and His is the praise, and He is Able to do all things."

Extended Declaration
لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ، لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ، لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، وَلَا نَعْبُدُ إِلَّا إِيَّاهُ، لَهُ النِّعْمَةُ وَلَهُ الْفَضْلُ وَلَهُ الثَّنَاءُ الْحَسَنُ، لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ مُخْلِصِينَ لَهُ الدِّينَ وَلَوْ كَرِهَ الْكَافِرُونَ
La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la shareeka lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamdu wa huwa 'ala kulli shay'in qadeer. La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah. La ilaha illallah, wa la na'budu illa iyyah, lahun-ni'matu wa lahul-fadlu wa lahuth-thana'ul-hasan. La ilaha illallahu mukhliseena lahud-deena wa law karihal-kafirun.
"There is no god but Allah alone, with no partner. His is the dominion and His is the praise, and He is Able to do all things. There is no power and no strength except with Allah. There is no god but Allah, and we worship none but Him. His is the blessing and His is the favour, and His is the good praise. There is no god but Allah, sincere to Him in religion even if the disbelievers hate it."
Muslim
La Hawla wa La Quwwata

La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah
"There is no power and no strength except with Allah."
Bukhari & Muslim — A treasure from the treasures of Paradise
Tasbeeh (33 + 33 + 33 + 1 = 100)
How to count: Say SubhanAllah 33 times, then Alhamdulillah 33 times, then Allahu Akbar 33 times. Finally, say the declaration of Tawheed once to complete 100. Whoever does this after every prayer, their sins will be forgiven even if they are like the foam of the sea. (Muslim) Other authentic variations of counting are also reported in the Sunnah.

Ayatul Kursi
اللَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ ۚ لَا تَأْخُذُهُ سِنَةٌ وَلَا نَوْمٌ ۚ لَهُ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الْأَرْضِ ۗ مَنْ ذَا الَّذِي يَشْفَعُ عِنْدَهُ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِهِ ۚ يَعْلَمُ مَا بَيْنَ أَيْدِيهِمْ وَمَا خَلْفَهُمْ ۖ وَلَا يُحِيطُونَ بِشَيْءٍ مِنْ عِلْمِهِ إِلَّا بِمَا شَاءَ ۚ وَسِعَ كُرْسِيُّهُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ ۖ وَلَا يَئُودُهُ حِفْظُهُمَا ۚ وَهُوَ الْعَلِيُّ الْعَظِيمُ
Allahu la ilaha illa Huwal-Hayyul-Qayyum. La ta'khudhuhu sinatun wa la nawm. Lahu ma fis-samawati wa ma fil-ard. Man dhal-ladhi yashfa'u 'indahu illa bi-idhnih. Ya'lamu ma bayna aydeehim wa ma khalfahum. Wa la yuheetuna bi shay'in min 'ilmihi illa bima sha'. Wasi'a kursiyyuhus-samawati wal-ard. Wa la ya'uduhu hifdhuhumaa. Wa Huwal-'Aliyyul-'Adheem.
"Allah! There is no god but He, the Living, the Self-Subsisting. Neither slumber overtakes Him nor sleep. To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth. Who is it that can intercede with Him except by His permission? He knows what is before them and what is behind them, and they encompass nothing of His knowledge except what He wills. His Kursi extends over the heavens and the earth, and He feels no fatigue in guarding them. And He is the Most High, the Most Great."
Quran 2:255 — Whoever recites this after every prayer, nothing prevents him from entering Paradise except death. (Nasa'i — Sahih)
The Three Quls (Surah Ikhlas, Falaq, Nas)
قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ...
Qul Huwa Allahu Ahad... Qul A'udhu bi Rabbil-Falaq... Qul A'udhu bi Rabbin-Nas...
Recite Surah Al-Ikhlas (112), Surah Al-Falaq (113), and Surah An-Nas (114) once after every obligatory prayer, and three times after Fajr and Maghrib.
Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi — Sahih
Special Adhkar for Fajr & Maghrib
After Fajr and Maghrib Only × 10
لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ يُحْيِي وَيُمِيتُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ
La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la shareeka lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamd, yuhyi wa yumeet, wa huwa 'ala kulli shay'in qadeer
"There is no god but Allah alone, with no partner. His is the dominion and His is the praise. He gives life and causes death, and He is Able to do all things."
Tirmidhi, Ahmad — Hasan
After Fajr and Maghrib Only × 7
اللَّهُمَّ أَجِرْنِي مِنَ النَّارِ
Allahumma ajirni minan-naar
"O Allah, save me from the Fire."
Abu Dawud — If one says this and dies that day/night, Allah will protect them from the Fire

Laylatul Qadr

▼
When: Seek it in the odd nights of the last ten nights of Ramadan (21st, 23rd, 25th, 27th, 29th).
The Recommended Dua for Laylatul Qadr
اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي
Allahumma innaka 'Afuwwun tuhibbul-'afwa fa'fu 'anni
"O Allah, You are Pardoning and You love to pardon, so pardon me."
Tirmidhi — Sahih
Context: Aisha (رضي الله عنها) asked the Prophet ﷺ: "If I know which night is Laylatul Qadr, what should I say during it?" He replied with this dua.

The 99 Names of Allah

▼
Virtue: The Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah has ninety-nine names — one hundred minus one. Whoever preserves them will enter Paradise." (Bukhari 7392 & Muslim 2677)
What does 'preserve' mean? Scholars explain it means: (1) memorising them, (2) understanding their meanings, (3) worshipping Allah by them, and (4) calling upon Him through them in supplication.

Common Errors in Salah

▼
Important: Correcting these errors will help perfect our prayer and earn greater reward. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Pray as you have seen me praying." (Bukhari)
The Intention
Saying the Intention Aloud
Saying "Nawaytu an usalliya..." out loud before prayer
The intention (niyyah) is made in the heart only, not spoken
Neither the Prophet ﷺ nor his Companions ever verbalised the intention. The intention is the resolve in the heart. — Ibn Taymiyyah, Majmu' al-Fatawa
Standing (Qiyam)
Not Straightening the Rows
Standing with gaps between worshippers or crooked rows
Stand shoulder to shoulder, foot to foot, with no gaps
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Straighten your rows, for straightening the rows is part of perfecting the prayer." (Bukhari & Muslim). He also said: "Straighten your rows or Allah will cause discord among your hearts." (Bukhari & Muslim)
Looking Around or Upward
Looking up at the sky, around the room, or at others during prayer
Keep your gaze at the place of prostration
The Prophet ﷺ said: "What is wrong with those people who look up at the sky during prayer?" His words became so severe that he said: "They should stop doing that, or else their eyesight will be taken away." (Bukhari)
Fidgeting and Unnecessary Movements
Playing with clothing, watch, phone, scratching, adjusting unnecessarily
Remain still and focused, moving only when necessary
The Prophet ﷺ saw a man adjusting his beard in prayer and said: "If his heart had khushu', his limbs would have khushu'." (Reported by scholars as a statement of the Salaf)
Ruku (Bowing)
Not Keeping the Back Straight
Bowing with a curved or arched back
Keep the back straight and level, head in line with the back
Abu Humayd رضي الله عنه described the Prophet's ﷺ prayer: "When he bowed, he kept his back straight, so that if water were poured on it, it would stay in place." (Ibn Majah — Sahih)
Not Pausing After Rising from Ruku
Going straight into sujood without standing fully upright
Stand completely upright and pause before going into sujood
The Prophet ﷺ said about the man who prayed badly: "Then stand up until you are standing straight." (Bukhari & Muslim). Standing still between ruku and sujood is a pillar of the prayer.
Sujood (Prostration)
Elbows on the Ground
Resting the forearms and elbows flat on the ground during sujood
Keep elbows raised off the ground, away from the sides
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Be balanced in sujood, and none of you should spread his forearms like a dog." (Bukhari & Muslim)
Not Prostrating on All Seven Limbs
Lifting feet off the ground, or forehead/nose not touching properly
Prostrate on forehead (with nose), both hands, both knees, and toes of both feet
The Prophet ﷺ said: "I have been commanded to prostrate on seven bones: the forehead — and he pointed to his nose — the two hands, the two knees, and the edges of the two feet." (Bukhari & Muslim)
Rushing Through Sujood
Pecking the ground quickly without settling
Take time in sujood, ensuring every bone settles in place
The Prophet ﷺ told the man who prayed badly: "Then prostrate until you are settled in your prostration." (Bukhari & Muslim)
Sitting Between Prostrations
Not Sitting Fully Between Prostrations
Rising from first sujood and going straight into the second without sitting
Sit upright between the two prostrations and be still
The Prophet ﷺ would sit between the two prostrations until every bone returned to its place. (Abu Dawud — Sahih)
General Errors
Rushing Through the Entire Prayer
Praying so fast that positions are not completed properly
Take time in each position, allowing every limb to settle
The Prophet ﷺ said to the man who rushed: "Go back and pray, for you have not prayed." He repeated this three times, then taught him to have tuma'neenah (tranquility) in each position. (Bukhari & Muslim)
Racing the Imam
Moving to the next position before or at the same time as the Imam
Follow the Imam, moving only after he has completed his movement
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Does not the one who raises his head before the Imam fear that Allah may transform his head into the head of a donkey, or his form into the form of a donkey?" (Bukhari & Muslim)
Crossing in Front of Someone Praying
Walking between a person and their place of prostration
Walk behind the person praying, or wait until they finish
The Prophet ﷺ said: "If the one who crosses in front of a person praying knew what (sin) was on him, he would stand for forty (years) rather than cross in front of him." (Bukhari & Muslim)
Not Using a Sutrah
Praying without anything in front to mark your prayer space
Pray towards a sutrah (barrier) such as a wall, pillar, or any object
The Prophet ﷺ said: "When one of you prays, let him pray towards a sutrah and get close to it." (Abu Dawud — Sahih)
Praying in Line with or Ahead of the Imam
Standing beside or in front of the Imam in congregation
Always stand behind the Imam, even if praying alone with him
When Ibn 'Abbas رضي الله عنهما stood beside the Prophet ﷺ, he moved him to stand behind him. (Bukhari & Muslim)
Remember: The prayer is the first deed we will be asked about on the Day of Judgement. Taking care to pray correctly is a sign of love for Allah and His Messenger ﷺ. May Allah help us perfect our prayers. آمين

The Farewell Sermon

▼
Khutbat al-Wada' — Delivered by the Prophet ﷺ on the 9th of Dhul Hijjah, 10 AH (6th March 632 CE), in the valley of Uranah at Mount Arafat, during his first and final Hajj, before more than 120,000 Companions.

O People —

Lend me an attentive ear, for I know not whether after this year, I shall ever be amongst you again. Therefore listen to what I am saying to you very carefully and take these words to those who could not be present here today.

O People —

Just as you regard this month, this day, this city as sacred, so regard the life and property of every Muslim as a sacred trust. Return the goods entrusted to you to their rightful owners. Hurt no one so that no one may hurt you. Remember that you will indeed meet your Lord, and that He will indeed reckon your deeds. Allah has forbidden you to take usury (interest); therefore all interest obligations shall henceforth be waived. Your capital, however, is yours to keep. You will neither inflict nor suffer any inequity.

Beware of Satan for the safety of your religion. He has lost all hope that he will ever be able to lead you astray in big things, so beware of following him in small things.

O People —

It is true that you have certain rights in regard to your women, but they also have rights over you. Remember that you have taken them as your wives only under Allah's trust and with His permission. Do treat your women well and be kind to them, for they are your partners and committed helpers.

O People —

Listen to me in earnest. Worship Allah, say your five daily prayers, fast during the month of Ramadan, and give your wealth in Zakat. Perform Hajj if you can afford to.

All mankind is from Adam and Eve. An Arab has no superiority over a non-Arab, nor a non-Arab has any superiority over an Arab; also a white has no superiority over a black, nor a black has any superiority over a white — except by piety and good action.

Learn that every Muslim is a brother to every Muslim and that the Muslims constitute one brotherhood. Nothing shall be legitimate to a Muslim which belongs to a fellow Muslim unless it was given freely and willingly. Do not, therefore, do injustice to yourselves.

Remember, one day you will appear before Allah and answer for your deeds. So beware, do not stray from the path of righteousness after I am gone.

O People —

No prophet or apostle will come after me, and no new faith will be born. Reason well therefore, O people, and understand words which I convey to you. I leave behind me two things, the Quran and my Sunnah, and if you follow these you will never go astray.

All those who listen to me shall pass on my words to others and those to others again; and may the last ones understand my words better than those who listened to me directly.

"Be my witness, O Allah, that I have conveyed Your message to Your people."

Note: The Farewell Sermon is narrated in multiple authentic reports across different collections. This presentation is a thematic compilation from various narrations, so the exact wording may differ between sources. Where a specific sentence is quoted, it should be traced to its individual narration and grading.
Note: The exact wording of the Farewell Sermon varies across authentic sources, as it was narrated by many Companions who each reported the portions they personally heard. Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal's Musnad contains the most complete version. Scholars have carefully compiled this composite from multiple authentic narrations.
Method note: This section is included for reminder and benefit. For strict verification, key statements should be presented with their individual hadith references and scholar gradings, rather than as one continuous sermon.
After concluding the sermon, the following verse was revealed: "This day I have perfected for you your religion, and completed My favour upon you, and have chosen for you Islam as your religion." (Quran 5:3)

Variations reported in the Sunnah are numerous. This guide presents commonly practised authentic forms.
