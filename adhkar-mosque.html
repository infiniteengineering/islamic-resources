Mosque & Adhān Adhkār

Remembrances for going to the mosque, entering, leaving, and responding to the adhān

Going to the Mosque
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 1 Leaving for Mosque
اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْ فِي قَلْبِي نُورًا ، وَفِي لِسَانِي نُورًا ، وَاجْعَلْ فِي سَمْعِي نُورًا ، وَاجْعَلْ فِي بَصَرِي نُورًا ، وَاجْعَلْ مِنْ خَلْفِي نُورًا ، وَمِنْ أَمَامِي نُورًا ، وَاجْعَلْ مِنْ فَوْقِي نُورًا ، وَمِنْ تَحْتِي نُورًا ، اللَّهُمَّ أَعْطِنِي نُورًا
Allāhumma j'al fī qalbī nūrā, wa fī lisānī nūrā, wa j'al fī sam'ī nūrā, wa j'al fī baṣarī nūrā, wa j'al min khalfī nūrā, wa min amāmī nūrā, wa j'al min fawqī nūrā, wa min taḥtī nūrā. Allāhumma a'ṭinī nūrā.
O Allah, place light in my heart, light in my tongue, place light in my hearing, place light in my sight, place light behind me, light in front of me, place light above me, and light below me. O Allah, grant me light.
Source: Al-Bukhari 6316 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Muslim 763 — Ṣaḥīḥ
Entering & Leaving the Mosque
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 2 Entering Mosque
Enter with the right foot and say:
أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ الْعَظِيمِ ، وَبِوَجْهِهِ الْكَرِيمِ ، وَسُلْطَانِهِ الْقَدِيمِ ، مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ الرَّجِيمِ
A'ūdhu billāhi-l-'aẓīm, wa bi-wajhihi-l-karīm, wa sulṭānihi-l-qadīm, mina-sh-shayṭāni-r-rajīm.
I seek refuge in Allah the Almighty, in His Noble Face, and His Eternal Authority, from the accursed Shayṭān.
When one says this, Shayṭān says: "He has been protected from me for the whole day."
Source: Abu Dawud 466 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī)
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 3 Entering Mosque
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ ، وَالصَّلَاةُ وَالسَّلَامُ عَلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ، اللَّهُمَّ افْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ
Bismillāh, wa-ṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-salāmu 'alā rasūli Allāh. Allāhumma ftaḥ lī abwāba raḥmatik.
In the Name of Allah, and prayers and peace be upon the Messenger of Allah. O Allah, open for me the gates of Your mercy.
Source: Muslim 713 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Abu Dawud 465 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī)
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 4 Leaving Mosque
Leave with the left foot and say:
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ ، وَالصَّلَاةُ وَالسَّلَامُ عَلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ، اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ فَضْلِكَ
Bismillāh, wa-ṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-salāmu 'alā rasūli Allāh. Allāhumma innī as'aluka min faḍlik.
In the Name of Allah, and prayers and peace be upon the Messenger of Allah. O Allah, I ask You from Your bounty.
Source: Muslim 713 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Ibn Majah 771 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī)
Responding to the Adhān
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Dhikr · 5 During Adhān
Repeat what the mu'adhdhin says, except when he says "Ḥayya 'ala-ṣ-ṣalāh" (Come to prayer) and "Ḥayya 'ala-l-falāḥ" (Come to success), say instead:
لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ
Lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāh.
There is no might nor power except with Allah.
Source: Al-Bukhari 611 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Muslim 383 — Ṣaḥīḥ
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 6 After Adhān
After the mu'adhdhin finishes, send ṣalawāt upon the Prophet ﷺ and then say:
اللَّهُمَّ رَبَّ هَذِهِ الدَّعْوَةِ التَّامَّةِ ، وَالصَّلَاةِ الْقَائِمَةِ ، آتِ مُحَمَّدًا الْوَسِيلَةَ وَالْفَضِيلَةَ ، وَابْعَثْهُ مَقَامًا مَحْمُودًا الَّذِي وَعَدْتَهُ
Allāhumma rabba hādhihi-d-da'wati-t-tāmmah, wa-ṣ-ṣalāti-l-qā'imah, āti Muḥammadan al-wasīlata wa-l-faḍīlah, wa-b'athu maqāman maḥmūdan alladhī wa'adtah.
O Allah, Lord of this perfect call and established prayer, grant Muḥammad the intercession and favor, and raise him to the praised station which You have promised him.
Whoever says this, my intercession will be guaranteed for him on the Day of Resurrection.
Source: Al-Bukhari 614 — Ṣaḥīḥ
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Dhikr · 7 Between Adhān & Iqāmah
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Supplication is not rejected between the adhān and the iqāmah, so make duʿā'."
Source: Abu Dawud 521 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī) · At-Tirmidhi 212 — Ṣaḥīḥ
While in the Mosque
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Dhikr · 8 Upon Entering Mosque
The Prophet ﷺ said: "When any one of you enters the mosque, let him not sit down until he prays two rakʿahs."
Source: Al-Bukhari 444 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Muslim 714 — Ṣaḥīḥ
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Dhikr · 9 Sitting in Mosque
The Prophet ﷺ said: "The angels send prayers upon any one of you so long as he remains in his place of prayer for which he made wuḍū', saying: 'O Allah, forgive him; O Allah, have mercy on him.'"
Source: Al-Bukhari 659 — Ṣaḥīḥ
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Dhikr · 10 Virtue of Walking to Mosque
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever purifies himself in his house, then comes to the mosque to perform an obligatory prayer, one of his steps erases a sin and the other raises him a degree."
Source: Muslim 666 — Ṣaḥīḥ
About This Collection

Variations exist in wording, frequency, and timing across authentic narrations. This collection presents widely accepted reports transmitted from the Prophet ﷺ and used by major scholars of hadith and Sunnah.
