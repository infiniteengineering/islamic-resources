Tarawih Prayer Adhkār

Night prayer in Ramadan — guidance from the Prophet ﷺ and supplications during Tarawih

The Practice of Tarawih
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Guidance · 1 Ramadan Nights
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever stands (in prayer) during Ramadan with faith and seeking reward, his previous sins will be forgiven." He ﷺ prayed eleven rakʿahs at night, including Witr.
Variations exist in the number of rakʿahs: eight rakʿahs plus three Witr (eleven total) was the Prophet's ﷺ regular practice. Twenty rakʿahs was practiced by the Companions during ʿUmar's caliphate. Both are authentic and accepted.
Source: Al-Bukhari 2009 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Muslim 759 — Ṣaḥīḥ (for the hadith about forgiveness)
Al-Bukhari 1147 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Muslim 738 — Ṣaḥīḥ (for eleven rakʿahs)
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Guidance · 2 Before Beginning
The intention for Tarawih is made in the heart. It is voluntary night prayer (qiyam) in Ramadan, prayed after ʿIshāʾ. No specific verbal intention is required — the intention is through the act of standing for prayer.
Principle: "Actions are by intentions" — Al-Bukhari 1 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Muslim 1907 — Ṣaḥīḥ
Between Sets of Rakʿahs
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Guidance · 3 Between Sets
It is recommended to rest briefly between sets of rakʿahs. During these breaks, the worshipper may sit quietly, make dhikr, or recite Qur'an. The Companions would rest for the duration it would take to pray four rakʿahs.
Source: Reported in authenticated compilations regarding the practice during ʿUmar's رضي الله عنه time
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 4 Between Sets (Optional)
سُبْحَانَ اللهِ وَالحَمْدُ للهِ وَلَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَاللهُ أَكْبَرُ
Subḥānallāh, wa-l-ḥamdu lillāh, wa lā ilāha illallāh, wallāhu akbar.
How perfect Allah is, and all praise is for Allah, and none has the right to be worshipped except Allah, and Allah is the Most Great.
General dhikr that can be said during breaks between prayer sets.
Source: At-Tirmidhi 3585 — Ṣaḥīḥ
During Tarawih Prayer
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Guidance · 5 During Prayer
The Prophet ﷺ would recite long portions of the Qur'an during night prayer. He would stand for extended periods, sometimes reciting an entire Juz' (section) in one rakʿah. However, he also said: "Pray as you have seen me praying" — meaning balance and moderation are important, especially when leading others.
Source: Al-Bukhari 631 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Muslim 772 — Ṣaḥīḥ
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Guidance · 6 During Recitation
When the Prophet ﷺ recited a verse mentioning mercy, he would pause and ask for mercy. When he recited a verse mentioning punishment, he would pause and seek refuge. When he recited a verse of glorification, he would glorify Allah.
Source: Muslim 772 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Abu Dawud 873 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī)
After Completing Tarawih
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 7 After Tarawih
After completing prayer, the same adhkār said after obligatory prayers may be recited. This includes:
أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللهَ
I seek Allah's forgiveness — 3 times
اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ السَّلَامُ وَمِنْكَ السَّلَامُ ، تَبَارَكْتَ يَا ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ
O Allah, You are Peace and from You is peace. Blessed are You, O Possessor of Majesty and Honor.
Source: Muslim 591 — Ṣaḥīḥ
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Guidance · 8 Concluding with Witr
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Make Witr the last of your prayer at night." Tarawih is followed by Witr prayer (one, three, five, seven, nine, or eleven rakʿahs). The Witr prayer has specific supplications covered in the dedicated Witr section.
Source: Al-Bukhari 998 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Muslim 751 — Ṣaḥīḥ
Additional Guidance
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Guidance · 9 At Home or Mosque
The Prophet ﷺ said: "The best prayer is a person's prayer in his home, except for the prescribed (obligatory) prayers." This includes Tarawih. However, praying in congregation at the mosque is also from the Sunnah and was practiced by the Companions. Both are excellent.
Source: Al-Bukhari 731 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Muslim 781 — Ṣaḥīḥ
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 10 Seeking Laylat al-Qadr
The Prophet ﷺ taught this supplication for Laylat al-Qadr (the Night of Decree), which is sought in the last ten nights of Ramadan during Tarawih and Qiyam:
اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي
Allāhumma innaka 'afuwwun tuḥibbu-l-'afwa fa-ʿfu 'annī.
O Allah, You are Most Forgiving, and You love forgiveness, so forgive me.
Source: At-Tirmidhi 3513 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Ibn Majah 3850 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī)
About This Collection

Variations exist in wording, frequency, and timing across authentic narrations. This collection presents widely accepted reports transmitted from the Prophet ﷺ and used by major scholars of hadith and Sunnah.
