قيام الليل

Qiyam al-Layl Adhkār

Night prayer (Tahajjud) — the voluntary prayer in the last third of the night, beloved to Allah

The Virtue of Night Prayer
QUR'AN
Virtue · 1 Allah's Praise
Allah describes the righteous servants in the Qur'an:
كَانُوا قَلِيلًا مِّنَ اللَّيْلِ مَا يَهْجَعُونَ ۝ وَبِالْأَسْحَارِ هُمْ يَسْتَغْفِرُونَ
"They used to sleep but little at night, and in the hours before dawn they would seek forgiveness."
Qur'an: Adh-Dhariyat 51:17-18
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Virtue · 2 Best Voluntary Prayer
The Prophet ﷺ said: "The best prayer after the obligatory prayers is the night prayer."
Source: Muslim 1163 — Ṣaḥīḥ
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Virtue · 3 Divine Descent
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Our Lord descends to the lowest heaven during the last third of every night and says: 'Who is calling upon Me that I may answer him? Who is asking from Me that I may give him? Who is seeking My forgiveness that I may forgive him?'"
Source: Al-Bukhari 1145 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Muslim 758 — Ṣaḥīḥ
Upon Waking for Night Prayer
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 4 Upon Waking at Night
This is one of the authentic wordings reported. The Prophet ﷺ would wake in the night and say:
اللَّهُمَّ لَكَ الحَمْدُ أَنْتَ نُورُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالأَرْضِ وَمَنْ فِيهِنَّ ، وَلَكَ الحَمْدُ أَنْتَ قَيِّمُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالأَرْضِ وَمَنْ فِيهِنَّ ، وَلَكَ الحَمْدُ أَنْتَ الحَقُّ وَوَعْدُكَ الحَقُّ ، وَقَوْلُكَ الحَقُّ ، وَلِقَاؤُكَ الحَقُّ ، وَالجَنَّةُ حَقٌّ ، وَالنَّارُ حَقٌّ ، وَالنَّبِيُّونَ حَقٌّ ، وَمُحَمَّدٌ حَقٌّ ، وَالسَّاعَةُ حَقٌّ ، اللَّهُمَّ لَكَ أَسْلَمْتُ ، وَبِكَ آمَنْتُ ، وَعَلَيْكَ تَوَكَّلْتُ ، وَإِلَيْكَ أَنَبْتُ ، وَبِكَ خَاصَمْتُ ، وَإِلَيْكَ حَاكَمْتُ ، فَاغْفِرْ لِي مَا قَدَّمْتُ وَمَا أَخَّرْتُ ، وَمَا أَسْرَرْتُ وَمَا أَعْلَنْتُ ، أَنْتَ المُقَدِّمُ وَأَنْتَ المُؤَخِّرُ ، لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ
Allāhumma laka-l-ḥamdu anta nūru-s-samāwāti wal-arḍi wa man fīhinn, wa laka-l-ḥamdu anta qayyimu-s-samāwāti wal-arḍi wa man fīhinn, wa laka-l-ḥamdu anta-l-ḥaqq, wa wa'duka-l-ḥaqq, wa qawluka-l-ḥaqq, wa liqā'uka-l-ḥaqq, wal-jannatu ḥaqq, wa-n-nāru ḥaqq, wa-n-nabiyyūna ḥaqq, wa Muḥammadun ḥaqq, wa-s-sā'atu ḥaqq. Allāhumma laka aslamtu, wa bika āmantu, wa 'alayka tawakkaltu, wa ilayka anabtu, wa bika khāṣamtu, wa ilayka ḥākamtu, fa-ghfir lī mā qaddamtu wa mā akhkhartu, wa mā asrartu wa mā a'lantu, anta-l-muqaddimu wa anta-l-mu'akhkhir, lā ilāha illā ant.
O Allah, to You is all praise — You are the Light of the heavens and the earth and all who are therein. To You is all praise — You are the Sustainer of the heavens and the earth and all who are therein. To You is all praise — You are the Truth, Your promise is true, Your word is true, meeting You is true, Paradise is true, the Fire is true, the Prophets are true, Muḥammad is true, and the Hour is true. O Allah, to You I have submitted, in You I have believed, upon You I have relied, to You I have turned, with Your help I have argued, and to You I refer judgement. So forgive me what I have sent forth and what I have left behind, what I have concealed and what I have declared. You are the One who brings forward and the One who puts back — none has the right to be worshipped except You.
Source: Al-Bukhari 1120 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Muslim 769 — Ṣaḥīḥ
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Guidance · 5 Upon Waking
When the Prophet ﷺ woke up at night, he would wipe the sleep from his face with his hand, then recite the last ten verses of Sūrah Āl ʿImrān (3:190-200), which begin with: "Indeed, in the creation of the heavens and the earth..."
Source: Al-Bukhari 183 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Muslim 763 — Ṣaḥīḥ
During Night Prayer
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 6 Opening Night Prayer
The Prophet ﷺ would begin his night prayer with:
اللَّهُمَّ رَبَّ جِبْرَائِيلَ وَمِيكَائِيلَ وَإِسْرَافِيلَ ، فَاطِرَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالأَرْضِ ، عَالِمَ الْغَيْبِ وَالشَّهَادَةِ ، أَنْتَ تَحْكُمُ بَيْنَ عِبَادِكَ فِيمَا كَانُوا فِيهِ يَخْتَلِفُونَ ، اهْدِنِي لِمَا اخْتُلِفَ فِيهِ مِنَ الْحَقِّ بِإِذْنِكَ ، إِنَّكَ تَهْدِي مَنْ تَشَاءُ إِلَى صِرَاطٍ مُسْتَقِيمٍ
Allāhumma rabba Jibrā'īla wa Mīkā'īla wa Isrāfīl, fāṭira-s-samāwāti wa-l-arḍ, 'ālima-l-ghaybi wa-sh-shahādah, anta taḥkumu bayna 'ibādika fīmā kānū fīhi yakhtalifūn, ihdinī limā khtulifa fīhi mina-l-ḥaqqi bi-idhnik, innaka tahdī man tashā'u ilā ṣirāṭin mustaqīm.
O Allah, Lord of Jibrīl, Mīkāʾīl, and Isrāfīl, Creator of the heavens and the earth, Knower of the unseen and the seen, You judge between Your servants concerning that in which they differ. Guide me to the truth in that which they differ, by Your permission. Indeed, You guide whom You will to the straight path.
Source: Muslim 770 — Ṣaḥīḥ
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Guidance · 7 During Recitation
The Prophet ﷺ would stand for long periods at night, reciting the Qur'an slowly and reflecting upon it. He would pause at verses of mercy to ask for mercy, at verses of punishment to seek refuge, and at verses of glorification to glorify Allah.
Source: Muslim 772 — Ṣaḥīḥ
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Guidance · 8 Weeping in Prayer
ʿAbdullah ibn al-Shikhkhīr رضي الله عنه said: "I saw the Messenger of Allah ﷺ praying, and there was a sound coming from his chest like the sound of a boiling kettle, on account of weeping."
Source: Abu Dawud 904 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī) · At-Tirmidhi 3858 — Ṣaḥīḥ
Seeking Forgiveness
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Guidance · 9 Before Fajr
The Prophet ﷺ said: "The closest that the Lord comes to a servant is in the last part of the night, so if you are able to be among those who remember Allah at that hour, then do so."
This is the time to seek forgiveness abundantly, as Allah praised those who seek forgiveness in the hours before dawn.
Source: At-Tirmidhi 3579 — Ṣaḥīḥ · An-Nasa'i 580 — Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī)
PROPHETIC SUPPLICATION
Dhikr · 10 Seeking Forgiveness
أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللهَ وَأَتُوبُ إِلَيْهِ
Astaghfirullāha wa atūbu ilayh.
I seek Allah's forgiveness and turn to Him in repentance.
Repeat this abundantly in the last third of the night and before Fajr.
Source: Al-Bukhari 6307 — Ṣaḥīḥ
After Completing Night Prayer
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Guidance · 11 Concluding Qiyam
Night prayer is concluded with Witr (the odd-numbered prayer). The Prophet ﷺ said: "Make Witr the last of your prayer at night." See the dedicated Witr section for specific supplications.
Source: Al-Bukhari 998 — Ṣaḥīḥ · Muslim 751 — Ṣaḥīḥ
PRACTICE OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
Guidance · 12 After Witr (Optional)
ʿĀ'ishah رضي الله عنها reported: "The Prophet ﷺ used to pray two brief rakʿahs after Witr while sitting." This is optional and was a specific practice of the Prophet ﷺ.
Source: Muslim 738 — Ṣaḥīḥ
About This Collection

Variations exist in wording, frequency, and timing across authentic narrations. This collection presents widely accepted reports transmitted from the Prophet ﷺ and used by major scholars of hadith and Sunnah.
